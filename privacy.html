Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

DDList ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website at ddlist.github.io. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use and disclosure of personal information when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to operate and improve DDList:

  • Usage data — anonymous analytics such as pages visited, referring URLs, browser type, device type and screen resolution. This data is collected automatically and cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • Contact information — if you email us or send a WhatsApp message, we receive your email address or phone number solely to respond to your enquiry.
  • Account information — if you sign in (e.g. via Google), we receive your display name, email address and profile photo. This is used only for authentication and is stored in Firebase Authentication.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate and maintain the website.
  • Analyze usage trends to improve content and performance.
  • Respond to your messages and support requests.
  • Prevent fraud and ensure security.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

DDList uses the following technologies:

  • Essential cookies — required for basic site functionality (e.g. theme preferences).
  • Google Analytics cookies — used to understand how visitors interact with the site. These cookies collect anonymous, aggregated data.
  • Local storage — your browser's local storage may be used to remember your theme preference and search filters.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Google Analytics — tracks and reports website traffic. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase (by Google) — provides authentication, hosting and database services. Firebase processes data in accordance with Firebase Privacy.
  • Google Fonts — serves the Figtree font. Google may collect font-request data. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website. GitHub collects server logs including IP addresses. See GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following cases:

  • When required by law or to protect our legal rights.
  • To service providers who assist in operating the website (hosting, analytics), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • In the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, with appropriate notice.

6. Data Retention

Analytics data is retained by Google Analytics for up to 26 months. Account data in Firebase is retained until you request deletion. Contact messages are retained only as long as necessary to address your enquiry.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics by using Google's opt-out tool or enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at shariqq.com@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

DDList is not directed to individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
