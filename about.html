About DDList

DDList is a free library of ready-to-use source code for developers, students, and hobbyists. The project offers Python scripts, Flutter templates, and HTML templates that you can download, study, and use in your own projects without any cost.

Every item in the collection comes with full source code so you can learn from it, customize it, or drop it straight into your own project. If you need help with installation, modification, or something entirely custom, you can reach out for freelance support.

Our Mission

DDList is built and maintained by a small team of developers passionate about open-source and knowledge sharing. We believe in giving back to the developer community by providing free, high-quality scripts and templates.

We develop our own scripts and tools, often taking inspiration from real-world projects and industry best practices. All code on DDList is original work created by our team.

Disclaimer

DDList does not distribute nulled, cracked, or pirated software. All scripts and templates on this platform are developed in-house by our team. We sometimes take inspiration from existing open-source projects and publicly available code, but all content is original work built from scratch.

We are committed to ethical software development and respecting intellectual property rights.
